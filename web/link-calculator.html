Equation and measurement plan

For a circular, unobscured diffraction-limited transmitter, the on-axis collected fraction at a centered circular receiver is η = 1 − J₀(x)² − J₁(x)², with x = π Dtx Drx / (2 λ R). At the tiny angles in this calculator, the small-argument approximation η ≈ x²/4 − x⁴/32 is numerically stable. Multiplying the emitted rate P λ / (h c) by η and the declared scalar efficiency gives the received count rate.

The 4.24 light-year reference is static and illustrative. An actual Earth–Proxima mission requires an epoch-frozen astrometric solution, moving receiver geometry, full spectral/pointing losses, measured detector count distributions and acquisition probabilities.
