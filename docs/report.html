01 / Distance and causality

A message must cross a physical path.

Emission is an event, not an animation frame

Define emission A as the instant a new randomized bit becomes physically available at the transmitter. Define reception B as the instant its value becomes locally decodable at the distant terminal. The ordinary Minkowski causal interval is

Δs² = −c² Δt² + Δx² + Δy² + Δz².

Our sign convention is (−,+,+,+). A spacelike separation has Δs² > 0 and cannot be connected by ordinary null or timelike future-directed signals. A coordinate speed or a faster processing algorithm is not evidence of a spacelike signal.

Two moving endpoints

In an inertial coordinate system, let Earth follow xE(t)=E₀+vEt and the distant terminal follow xB(t)=B₀+vBt. The one-way null intercept τ satisfies:

|B(t₀+τ) − E(t₀)|² = c² τ².

After a reply-generation delay τp, solve a second null intercept from the then-current remote location to the then-current Earth worldline. The resulting round trip is not generally twice an instantaneous distance divided by c. The static illustrative 4.24-light-year baseline nevertheless yields about 8.48 years for an immediate reply.

Inertial 3D implementation, point-ahead and uncertainty sampling

Scope and precision

Constant-velocity worldlines are a validation baseline, not a frozen astronomical ephemeris. This code does not yet solve light propagation in a curved Solar/Galactic metric, fit Gaia astrometric covariance, model acceleration or calibrate clock transfer. Those omissions must be closed before issuing a physical Earth–Proxima pointing prescription.

02 / Optical channel and modulation

Convert joules into detected information.

Small-receiver diffraction benchmark

For an unobstructed circular transmitter, a receiver much smaller than the far-field diffraction spot, a monochromatic carrier and ideal acquisition, the approximate captured power fraction is

z = π Dt Dr / (2 λ R),
ηcapture ≈ z²/4 − z⁴/32.

The mean detected photon rate is

Ṅ ≈ (Pt λ / hc) · ηcapture · ηsystem.

These equations do not include telescope obscuration, imperfect wavefronts, thermal stability, optical spectral losses, pointing acquisition or detector dead time. In the live site, pointing jitter uses a separate Gaussian-equivalent approximation and must not be mistaken for an exact jitter-averaged Airy integral.

Source and regression tests

Photon statistics determine symbol decisions

With M-ary pulse-position modulation, one of M intervals contains the intended signal pulse. In an independent Poisson model, the selected slot follows Poisson(s+b) and each other slot follows Poisson(b). For b>0, the ideal hard-decision maximum-likelihood detector selects the largest observed count.

Non ~ Poisson(s+b)
Noff ~ Poisson(b)

If background is exactly zero, the symbol error probability with random guessing after a no-photon event is

PSER = (1 − 1/M) exp(−s).

This is an uncoded symbol error rate—not bit error rate or delivered frame throughput. The Python programme also includes seeded count simulation, 95% Wilson intervals, a simple two-axis pointing-jitter model and separate soft-decision/FEC experiments.

Reproduce the stochastic model

Where the modulation research goes next

A deployable link requires spectral stellar background estimates, detector quantum efficiency, clock recovery, acquisition, point-ahead errors, bandwidth, finite peak optical power, blocking/guard slots, CCSDS-consistent synchronization, soft information and a measured coded frame-error curve. No modulation family is assumed globally optimal; compare goodput per joule and accepted new bits per received photon across real operating conditions.

Low-voltage teaching experiment: optoelectronic loopback

Digital 3.3 V output → 330 Ω series resistor → visible LED → ground. Across a short enclosed air gap, place a phototransistor with emitter to receiver ground. Pull its collector/sense node up to 3.3 V with 10 kΩ and pass the signal into a compatible Schmitt-trigger input. Light should pull the sense node toward LOW; verify actual logic polarity and response delay.

ILED ≈ (3.3 − 2.0) V / 330 Ω ≈ 3.9 mA

This current assumes a 2.0 V forward drop; select components using actual manufacturer ratings. **Do not** connect high-voltage avalanche circuitry or a high-power laser to this reference setup.

04 / Quantum control

Correlated photons are not a remote keyboard.

A Bell pair gives the wrong kind of knowledge

For the Bell state |Φ⁺⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2, the receiver's reduced state is one-half the identity. Alice can choose a measurement basis, but Bob's unread local probabilities do not reveal that choice.

ρB = TrA(|Φ⁺⟩⟨Φ⁺|) = I/2
P(B=0 | Alice chooses X) = 1/2
P(B=0 | Alice chooses Z) = 1/2

Two observers discover the joint pattern only after bringing their records together. Quantum teleportation has a classical communication requirement. A seeded Python null control and deliberate ordinary-leak positive control are available in the repo.

See full derivation and experiment

What could invalidate a no-signalling conclusion?

Unsynchronized time windows, post-selection, nonrandom or predictable bit choice, detector drift, a common source, hidden classical leakage or a wrongly specified causal separation can all create apparent associations. A statistically significant mutual information estimate is not proof of causal signalling.

For a genuine novel channel the relevant statistic must be measured from receiver-only data recorded before any allowed classical channel could bring the fresh message, with time-correlated null surrogates and independent replication.

05 / Speculative gravitation, isolated from the hardware baseline

A spacetime shortcut needs a physical spacetime.

Suppose a proposed metric gμν appears to connect events faster than a reference flat-space photon. That does not prove a signal outside the actual metric's light cone. The null structure, stress-energy and global causal relationships must be computed for the candidate geometry itself.

ds² = gμν dxμ dxν
Gμν + Λgμν = (8πG/c⁴) Tμν
gμν kμkν = 0 (null rays)

Physical scrutiny includes conservation ∇μTμν=0, observer-dependent energy density, null/weak energy conditions where relevant, matter source dynamics, stability, chronology, and whether a usable corridor can be established in finite time. Some conditions are theory-specific; do not assume that a failure or success of one energy condition establishes the whole physical argument.

The Alcubierre warp geometry, Krasnikov-type metric, traversable wormhole and Hyperwave proposal occupy a theoretical hypothesis ledger. They are not advanced stages of the LED or photon-counting electronics. This repo has no working instrument that creates such a metric.

What has not been achieved

We have not built a space-qualified laser terminal, commissioned a single-photon receiver, frozen an astrometric Earth–Proxima solution, demonstrated measured end-to-end performance, or discovered controllable faster-than-light information transfer. This report is a foundation for those research questions.
