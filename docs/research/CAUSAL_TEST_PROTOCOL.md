# Falsifiable protocol for superluminal *information* claims
**Version:** 0.1 | **Status:** preregistration template; no experiment conducted | **Investigator:** Biswajit Jana

## Definition first: actual causal structure, not an apparent coordinate speed

Let transmitter event A contain the generation of a newly sampled, authenticated random message X. Receiver event B is timestamped when Y becomes locally decodable. For a specified measured or modelled physical spacetime (M,g), the strong signal-causality question is whether B lies outside J_plus(A), the **actual metric's future causal set**. In a fixed Minkowski comparison, a necessary spacelike indicator is c^2(dt)^2 - ||dx||^2 < 0; this is not a globally sufficient test for an arbitrary curved metric or a metric whose sources are still hypothetical.

An early arrival against a *reference/background* Minkowski light-travel time does not establish causal violation if a candidate curved metric has a shorter physical null route. Before any FTL assertion, calculate causal relationships in the candidate **actual** g_(mu nu), and rule out the carrier, clock, path or synchronization being mischaracterized.

## Null and alternative

H0: Without an allowed ordinary timelike/null channel, intervention on the sender's fresh X does not alter the receiver-only pre-causal-horizon Y distribution; I(X;Y_pre | predeclared side information) = 0. The receiver can exhibit correlations due to common pre-shared data, but not selectable fresh information.

H1: Fresh sender-selected X changes receiver-only Y **outside** J_plus(A), after all permitted pre-shared state has been conditioned on or controlled, side channels eliminated and independent replication performed.

A low permutation p-value alone establishes neither physical propagation nor a valid definition of spacelike separation.

## Research instrument and experimental controls

1. **Blind, last-moment message selection:** Generate random X only after both endpoint devices are sealed, clock sync is frozen, and the legal causal horizon is predeclared. Cryptographically commit to seeds and preserve sealed, append-only hardware event logs; keep secrets private until analysis.
2. **Clock and distance audit:** Trace clock calibration; measure event times in an explicit reference frame; conservatively propagate timing, spatial and ephemeris covariance. Record lower uncertainty bound on what counts as a spacelike/causally disconnected interval.
3. **Separated intervention:** Randomize transmit/quiet/decoy conditions; demonstrate the receiver cannot infer transmit condition from shared power supplies, cables, clock triggers, electromagnetic leakage, storage/network services or accidentally time-correlated schedules.
4. **Strict pre-horizon cut:** Filter data using a *predeclared* causal arrival boundary. No post-hoc selection, future conditioning, leaked message file, shared predictors or delayed classical disclosure.
5. **Receiver-only decoding:** Lock the decoder before seeing test labels; measure BER, full confusion matrix and conditional information, not just one favorable correlation. Report runs with no decoded packet.
6. **Null distribution:** Use time-preserving permutation/block permutations, synthetic independent controls, negative-message controls, repeated seeded baselines and independent blind holdout days. Reuse no random seeds across physical trials. Correct multiple comparisons; preregister alpha and minimally important effect.
7. **Positive controls:** Show the same payload decoded over an ordinary optical/RF path only *after* its valid light-time. Test a deliberately leaky side channel and demonstrate the leak audit detects it.
8. **Independent replication:** Give an outside team instrument schematics, code, and preregistered recipe; they must reproduce on independently instrumented hardware before any extraordinary claim.
9. **Chronology:** For any controllable superluminal transport candidate, test reversed-message order in transformed frames and examine whether the transport permits a tachyonic antitelephone or closed causal curve. Requiring physical global causality is additional to local timing tests.

## Speculative GR validation gates

- Show the exact metric g_mu_nu, signature, units, all asymptotic/initial/boundary conditions, nondegeneracy, causal orientation and source-support region.
- Independently derive the connection, Riemann and Ricci tensors, scalar curvature, Einstein tensor, conservation (covariant divergence of T), and compare two implementations in simple analytic limits.
- Infer T_mu_nu = c^4(G_mu_nu + Lambda g_mu_nu)/(8 pi G) for a stated convention; do not substitute a metric-only stress tensor for a realizable laboratory matter model.
- Quantify WEC/NEC/DEC/SEC for observer/null-direction families that are not restricted to a few convenient directions; a positive Eulerian observer value alone does not establish WEC.
- Require known or explicitly hypothetical constitutive/source dynamics; check stability, causal control of creation/removal, energy budgets, quantum energy bounds, cutoff regime, and local/global chronology.
- Reproduce literature prior art ([Hyperwave](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12069), [warp-drive taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.06824), [NEC critique](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03079)) before claiming a new technique. The literature includes mathematical metrics; it does not provide a validated manufacturable FTL transceiver.

## Deliverable classification

Every finding gets one label: **established measurement**, **source-grounded calculation**, **reproduced simulation**, **unvalidated hypothesis**, or **falsified hypothesis**. Maintain metric definition, source URL/DOI, numerical parameters, source version, software commit, uncertainties, and blinded-trial metadata.

**Present conclusion:** No verified transport of newly chosen information outside the actual future light cone. The project is an open falsification-and-design programme, not a proven FTL signalling device.
